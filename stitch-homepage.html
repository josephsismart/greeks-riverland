Greeks of the Riverland

Families, stories and memories from South Australia’s Greek community

1950 – 2020

Greek family fruit growers, Renmark, 1965

(Kalantzis and Vlachos families)

A community far from home.
A story worth remembering.

From the 1950s onward, Greek migrants and their families became an important part of life in South Australia’s Riverland. They worked in orchards, packing sheds, factories, shops and small businesses. They raised families, supported one another and created a close-knit community.

This project has been created to record the families, photographs, memories and stories of the Greek community of the Riverland, so that their history is not forgotten.
